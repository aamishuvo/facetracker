FaceTracker

On-device face & expression analysis

What this is. A demo that runs four small neural networks inside your browser tab to find faces and guess apparent age and expression. Your video never leaves your device — there is no server, no upload, and no recording. What it is not. It cannot identify anyone, and it cannot detect intent, honesty, or criminality. How it works

Everything happens in this tab. When you enable the camera or open a clip, four models are fetched once from this site and then run against each sampled frame using WebGL on your GPU:

  1. TinyFaceDetector — finds face bounding boxes.
  2. FaceLandmark68 — places 68 landmark points, used to align each face.
  3. FaceExpressionNet — scores 7 expression classes on the aligned crop.
  4. AgeNet — regresses an apparent-age estimate.

There is no backend. Open your browser's network panel: after the model files load, the page makes no further requests. That is the whole privacy story — not a policy promise, an architectural one.

Three things the numbers do not mean

Age is a guess about appearance, not a birthday. Published error for this class of model runs roughly ±4–8 years, and it is worst on the faces least represented in its training data — children, older adults, and darker skin tones. Treat the range, not the number.

"Expression" is not "emotion". The model scores what a face is doing. A large body of affective-science research finds that facial configurations map only weakly and inconsistently onto what a person actually feels, and that the mapping varies by person, culture and context. A "happy" reading means the mouth corners are raised. It does not mean the person is happy.

Nothing here infers intent. No face model can tell you what someone plans to do. That is not a gap this demo will fill later; it is a claim with no scientific basis.

Deliberate omissions

The face-recognition model that ships with this library is not included in this repository, so the app cannot generate identity embeddings, match a face against a watchlist, or link the same person across frames or sessions. The library's binary gender classifier is loaded as part of the age model but is never displayed: it is a two-class output that misclassifies trans and non-binary people by construction and adds nothing to the demo.
